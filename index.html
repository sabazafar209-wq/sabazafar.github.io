Welcome

Welcome to my portfolio. I am a Data Analyst with a strong foundation in Computer Science and a passion for transforming data into meaningful insights. I analyze datasets, build interactive dashboards and present clear, data-driven findings that support effective decision-making.

Alongside my dashboard work, I research learning analytics and explainable AI in education. I have published two open-access preprints on modeling learner engagement and on explainable analytics for teachers.

I invite you to explore my projects and research, where I demonstrate practical skills in data cleaning, visualization, machine learning and analysis.
