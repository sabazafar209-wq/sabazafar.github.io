Research & Publications

Learning analytics · Explainable AI in education · Digital competence in teaching

Research Focus

My research studies how learner data can support teachers and learners in digital environments. I build machine-learning models of engagement and cognitive load, explain their predictions so that teachers can act on them, and examine how teachers of different ages adopt digital tools.

Preprints

Both papers are solo-authored, open access on OSF and not yet peer reviewed.

Toward Estimating Cognitive Load and Engagement in Digital Learning Environments Using Behavioral Learning Analytics

Zafar, S. (2026) · OSF Preprints · doi:10.35542/osf.io/bgnkw_v1

Trains random forest and LSTM models on clickstream data from the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset (OULAD) to estimate learners' engagement and cognitive load, then explains the predictions with SHAP. The paper reports corrected and non-replicating results openly.

Read on OSF

Explainable and Age-Aware Learning Analytics for Teacher-Facing Systems: A Synthesis and Position Paper

Zafar, S. (2026) · OSF Preprints · doi:10.35542/osf.io/25m96_v1

Argues that learning analytics tools built for teachers should explain their outputs and account for how teachers of different ages use digital technology.
